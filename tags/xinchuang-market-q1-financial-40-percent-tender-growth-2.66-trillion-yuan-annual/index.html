China Open Source Daily — 2026-10-11

Two institutionally dense placements, both from the 2026-10-10 WeChat input feed, sitting at institutional layers this series’ prior ten October briefings have not documented at before. (1) The openEuler Community’s September 2026 operations report, released through OpenAtom Foundation’s openEuler official channel on 2026-09-30 — first-documented instance in this series of a Chinese-open-source-community operational-report surface being operationalized at the emergent-community-scale layer simultaneously with the administrator-facing community-layering layer: 10 million+ end users, 29,000+ developers, 2,199 organization members, 112 SIG groups, 340,800 cumulative PRs, 167,500 Issues, 6,225,400 Comments — a dataset that, in aggregate, is not a set of administrative-construction KPIs but a set of emergent-ecosystem operations indicators. Paired with Huawei’s Wang Tao HC 2026 keynote statement (“openEuler, after seven years, has surpassed 20 million installed instances and holds the #1 China server operating system share”) and this series’ prior Aug 8, Aug 25, Sep 11, Sep 23, and Oct 4 briefings’ documentation of the openEuler surface at the administrative-open-source-and-administrator-facing-open-source layer — the pair completes the institutional object at the emergent-ecosystem-operations layer. (2) The State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council Decree No. 46 — Measures for Investigating and Holding Accountable for Violations in Central Enterprise Operations and Investment — released through a domestic-database outlet on 2026-10-10 — first-documented instance in this series of the Xinchuang-southeast-side-of-stratification institutional surface being operationalized at the lifetime-reckoning-and-personalized-reckoning layer simultaneously with the cobra-effect-plus-forgery-technology-evolution layer: Article 4 lifetime accountability for major decisions, Article 35 collective-decision members held separately (collective decision is not an exemption), Article 51 post-retirement/post-transfer/post-resignation accountability retained with compensation obligation for state-asset loss, direct-responsible-person 100% annual performance pay deduction plus 100% term incentive deduction plus three-year medium-and-long-term incentive forfeit, and severe cases leading to demotion plus lifetime prohibition from state-owned enterprise service. Paired with the June 2026 MIIT-CAC-Central-Purchasing-Procurement-Center new rules (government, party, state-informatization new-and-modified systems require full-stack Xinchuang adaptation plus code-chain-full-link audit, non-compliant systems are disqualified from bidding and acceptance) and the State Council Office Document No. 2025-34 domestic-product standard (domestic products enjoy bidding price-review preference from this year) — three policy lines tighten into a single accountability surface. SASAC 79 Instruction targets end-of-2027 Xinchuang transformation completion; only slightly more than a year remains. Financial Xinchuang tender scale rose more than 40 percent year-on-year in Q1; annual Xinchuang market projected at 2.66 trillion yuan. Volume grows; the number of people willing to sign drops. Xinchuang selection logic inverts from “cheapest and pretty-parameter” to “won’t get in trouble” — evidence chain (source code, audit report, national-testing evaluation level, real deployment cases) shifts from bonus item to hard currency. The piece explicitly names this as the cobra effect — Yangquan’s 1980s horse-skin bounty case where people killed healthy horses to sell the hide — generalized to Xinchuang policy as the incentive for forgery technology to evolve alongside the accountability regime. One perspective, not a verdict.
