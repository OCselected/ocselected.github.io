China Open Source Daily — 2026-11-02

On 2026-10-19 Qin Shuo published a 12,000-character investigation into the Chinese software industry, built on five firm interviews and Juyuan data: the 243 A-share listed B2B software companies grew revenue 7.9 percent across 2021-2025 while deducted non-recurring profit went from plus 17.89 billion yuan to minus 17.68 billion, and the share of loss-makers went from one in five in 2021 to 64.7 percent in H1 2026. In it is one sentence’s worth of institutional evidence that carries further than any aggregate: a provincial Department of Finance e-mall capped an IT package at 7.672 million yuan for a system serving 150,000 concurrent users across three administrative levels, and awarded it for 10 yuan. Verified for this briefing: the Ministry of Finance’s Caiku [2026] No. 2 notice, effective 2026-02-01, really does establish a four-threshold abnormal-low-price review at 50, 50, 45 and 65 percent, and really does apply outside the government-procurement domain nowhere. Qin diagnoses four mechanisms - lowest-price award driven by procurement officers’ fear of being blamed for choosing better, extended payment cycles, project-based rather than subscription business models, and SOE digital subsidiaries that take internal projects and subcontract the hard implementation work downward - and his five remedies, including redefining zizhu kekong from in-house development to substitutability and sustainable service, are a list of re-separations of a buyer, a rule-maker and a supplier that the Chinese state currently occupies all three of at once. The argument the article never makes: every property it asks procurement to reward is a property FLOSS licences are engineered to produce.

China Open Source Daily — 2026-11-01

On 2026-09-18 Beijing’s Municipal Bureau of Economy and Information Technology and its Development and Reform Commission issued the Action Plan for Accelerating the Development of the Token Economy (2026-2028) — six dimensions, ten measures, first provincial-level token-economy policy in the country. Its two most institutional proposals are not subsidies: a graded evaluation standard for ’token factories’ scored on model-adaptation count, token throughput, first-token latency, cache hit rate and PUE; and a token distribution platform to be written into the new-infrastructure plan, offering developers one interface, model aggregation and metered settlement. Set beside that, two financing schemes already in operation: Beijing E-Town’s 2-billion-yuan token loans closed in mid-September through six banks, and Haizhu district’s guarantee-innovation product from August — both using token production and consumption as a credit signal for firms with high R&D and no collateral. And a consumer layer nobody planned: token packs sold like mobile data, token credit-card rewards, tokens handed out at dumpling restaurants, tokens resold on Xianyu, against a State Data Administration series that went from 100 billion daily tokens in early 2024 to 500 trillion by May 2026. The economics being assembled here is older than it looks: 词元 — word-plus-currency — was adopted as the official term in March 2026, and the last Chinese government to make a unit of consumption into a unit of account ran into a problem that Mises described in 1912 and that no rediscount window ever solved.

China Open Source Daily — 2026-10-30

On 2026-10-24, at the 2026 Global Governance Forum in Xiongan, Foreign Minister Wang Yi released the two-year progress report of China’s AI Capacity Building Inclusive Plan, issued by the Global AI Innovation Governance Centre on the Ministry of Foreign Affairs domain. Read end to end it is not a technical document. Its ten actions carry ten numbered cases, and exactly one of them is an open-source artefact — FlagOS, a multi-chip system software stack supporting 21 chip vendors and 1,454 operators. The other nine are platforms, deployments, seminars, toolkits and statements, culminating in WAICO, signed at Shanghai on 2026-07-16 by 29 states and now holding 38 founding members, described in the report as the first intergovernmental organisation in the AI field. The document’s fourth lesson states the thesis plainly — openness is the precondition for inclusive development — and then, in the same paragraph, says what is missing: toolchains, test environments and related support that remain to be built. Verified against the report’s own inventory, and set beside two documents from the same fortnight at other registers: a MOFCOM think tank arguing in China Daily on 2026-10-13 that permissive licences and stable release cadence, not platform-building, are what produced Qwen’s 150,000-plus derivatives; and the 2026-10-20 State Council Information Office briefing where MIIT reported the 10-billion-download figure that this series first read on 2026-10-06 as 100 billion. That discrepancy is not a correction but an instruction in how these numbers are made — and it is the reason the checkable question below is about repositories, not figures.
