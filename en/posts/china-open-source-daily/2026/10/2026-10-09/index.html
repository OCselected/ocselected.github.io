China Open Source Daily — 2026-10-09#

🏗️ Institutional Change — OpenHarmony’s 《生成式AI工具使用与开源贡献策略》 (Generative AI Tool Use and Open Source Contribution Strategy) Published as the First Large-Chinese-OS-Community Official AI-Tool-Use Rulebook For Open-Source Contributions#

1. 开放原子开源基金会 (OpenAtom Foundation) / 开源鸿蒙社区 (OpenHarmony Community) (2026-10-08) — 《生成式AI工具使用与开源贡献策略》 officially released

2. OpenHarmony Community WeChat official account — “开源鸿蒙《生成式AI工具使用与开源贡献策略》正式发布” (OpenHarmony 《Generative AI Tool Use and Open Source Contribution Strategy》 Officially Released)

3. Community rulebook — eight sections: (i) Basic Principles (人类主导/责任到人/安全优先/合规必达 — human-led, accountability-to-person, security-first, compliance-mandatory); (ii) Permitted AI-Assistance Scenarios (code-writing, documentation translation, test-case authoring, problem-analysis, patch-explanation — five permitted scenarios); (iii) Disclosure Norms (four categories that must be disclosed + four categories exempt from disclosure + heightened requirements for high-sensitivity modules); (iv) Six Prohibited Behaviors (unverified AI outputs, fabricated test data, batch-issue-PR flooding, uploading sensitive data to AI, sole-reliance on AI for code review, unapproved AI-agent deployment); (v) License / Privacy / Data controls; (vi) Issue / Bug / Security-Vulnerability special rules; (vii) Reviewer-side AI-review rules; (viii) Community-enforcement and policy-iteration mechanism.

4. Rulebook’s sharpest institutional provisions: (a) Maintainers may demand code-contribution submission withdrawal if the contributor cannot explain AI-generated code’s logic, risk, or license provenance; (b) High-severity vulnerabilities require two-person cross-verification — AI-inference is not permitted to determine risk-grade; (c) Undisclosed AI contribution with a security risk triggers PR closure or PR take-back; (d) Four-dimensional governance stack: manual review + full test suite + transparent disclosure + tiered verification.

5. Rulebook’s positioning claim (community channel’s own metadata): “The world’s first generative-AI-tool-use rulebook officially issued by a large operating-system open-source community.”

OpenHarmony’s October 8, 2026 publication of the 《生成式AI工具使用与开源贡献策略》 is the first-documented instance in this series of a large Chinese operating-system open-source community’s official community channel publishing a formal AI-tool-use rulebook for open-source contributions at the AI-governance-rule-execution layer simultaneously with this series’ September 8 briefing’s 14-billion-device-OpenHarmony-base-layer-plus-90-to-70-percent-contribution-share-decline-plus-Hubble-20-percent-Huawei-vendor-arm-stake institutional object and this series’ September 14 briefing’s state-directed-open-source-project-selection-plus-independent-open-source-license-compliance-guidance-plus-community-first-release-plus-research-evaluation-incorporation-plus-quantitative-project-target institutional object — a two-surface-simultaneous AI-governance-rule-execution institutional-form observation.

The sharpest institutional-economics fact of the day: OpenHarmony’s official community channel publishes a formal AI-tool-use rulebook for open-source contributions that names four institutional principles (人类主导 / 责任到人 / 安全优先 / 合规必达 — human-led, accountability-to-person, security-first, compliance-mandatory) as the operative governance frame, and operationalizes three Williamson L3 governance mechanisms: (a) mandatory disclosure with four categories of mandatory-disclosure and four categories of exempt-from-disclosure scenarios; (b) two-person cross-verification for high-severity vulnerabilities, with an explicit prohibition on AI-inference directly determining risk-grade; (c) maintainer-side right to demand code-contribution withdrawal if the contributor cannot explain the logic, risk, or license provenance of AI-generated code. The rulebook is not a technical standard — it is an L3 governance mechanism that operationalizes the community’s own AI-tool-use rule surface as a community-governance instrument rather than a platform-vendor instrument. The OpenHarmony community’s positioning claim that this is “the world’s first generative-AI-tool-use rulebook officially issued by a large operating-system open-source community” is an institutional-positioning claim by the community channel itself, not an external verification — but the claim’s institutional significance is that a large Chinese open-source community is formalizing a rule surface at the community level, at the same institutional layer at which this series’ September 14 briefing documented the state-directed AI+Software-Special-Action-Implementation-Plan at the national-industrial-policy layer.

The AI-assistance-disclosure layer. Per the community rulebook: the four mandatory-disclosure categories are (i) core-logic AI-generated code with a high share; (ii) test suites originating from AI; (iii) large-scale cross-module refactoring performed by AI; (iv) security reports dependent on AI inference. The four exempt-from-disclosure categories are (i) spelling and grammar polish; (ii) single-line IDE autocomplete; (iii) code formatting; (iv) comment polishing. High-sensitivity modules receive heightened requirements — mandatory security self-checklist, mandatory second-verification reviewer, mandatory security test-case list. The institutional reading is that the AI-assistance-disclosure layer is being operationalized at the community-governance-plus-reviewer-side-rules institutional-form layer — the community is being asked to draw a specific boundary between AI-assisted-contributions-that-require-disclosure and AI-assisted-contributions-that-do-not, and the boundary is being drawn at the community level rather than at the platform-vendor level (GitHub’s Copilot policy, Apache’s AI policy) — a structurally distinct institutional-form object from the Western-platform-vendor-instrument layer.

The six-prohibited-behaviors layer. Per the community rulebook: (i) unverified AI outputs; (ii) fabricated test data; (iii) batch-issue-PR flooding; (iv) uploading sensitive data to public AI systems; (v) sole-reliance on AI for code review; (vi) unapproved deployment of AI agents. The institutional reading is that the six-prohibited-behaviors layer is being operationalized at the community-governance-plus-reviewer-side-rules-plus-CI-gate institutional-form layer simultaneously with the AI-assistance-disclosure layer — a two-layer-simultaneous AI-governance institutional-form observation. The prohibition on uploading sensitive data to public AI systems — specifically “未公开内核不上传公有AI” (unpublished kernel is not to be uploaded to public AI) — is a first-documented instance in this series of a large Chinese OS-community placing a public-AI-system-uploading prohibition on unpublished-kernel-code contributions at the community-governance-plus-reviewer-side-rules layer simultaneously with this series’ October 1 briefing’s documentation of the open-source-label-as-market-positioning-plus-Xinchuang-market-instrument layer and this series’ October 5 briefing’s Model-Diplomat-MOFCOM-plus-NDRC-tiered-AI-model-export-control layer.

The two-person-cross-verification-and-prohibition-on-AI-inference-directly-determining-risk-grade layer. Per the community rulebook: “AI仅辅助整理日志Issue必须人工核验禁止AI自动提交Issue” (AI only assists in log organization, issues must be manually verified, AI automatic issue-submission is prohibited); “AI推理不能直接定级风险” (AI inference cannot directly determine risk-grade); “高危漏洞需两人以上交叉验证” (high-severity vulnerabilities require two-person cross-verification); “未公开漏洞不输入公有AI” (undisclosed vulnerabilities must not be input into public AI). The institutional reading is that the two-person-cross-verification-and-prohibition-on-AI-inference-directly-determining-risk-grade layer is being operationalized at the community-governance-plus-reviewer-side-rules institutional-form layer — a structurally distinct institutional-form object from the Western-platform-vendor-instrument layer this series’ prior briefings’ Western-foundation-governance-seat layer (PyTorch Foundation, September 10 briefing; AAIF, September 7 briefing) documented.

The institutional-economics question this cycle’s briefing documents but does not answer. Whether the OpenHarmony community channel’s formalization of a community-level AI-tool-use rulebook converts the OpenHarmony community’s own institutional-form object (this series’ September 8 briefing’s 14-billion-device-plus-90-to-70-percent-contribution-share-decline-plus-Hubble-20-percent-stake-plus-2028-ecosystem-platform-2028-target) into a community-governance-instrument-object rather than a platform-vendor-instrument-object, and whether the same AI-governance-rule-execution surface is being operationalized at the community level simultaneously with the same state-directed-industrial-policy surface this series’ September 14 briefing documented at the national-industrial-policy layer.

Institutional significance: OpenHarmony’s 《生成式AI工具使用与开源贡献策略》 is the first-documented instance in this series of a large Chinese operating-system open-source community’s official community channel being operationalized at the AI-governance-rule-execution layer simultaneously with this series’ September 8 briefing’s OpenHarmony-14-billion-device-base-layer-plus-90-to-70-percent-contribution-share-decline layer and this series’ September 14 briefing’s state-directed-open-source-project-selection-and-community-first-release layer — a two-surface-simultaneous AI-governance-rule-execution institutional-form observation.

🏗️ Institutional Unwinding — 国产信创外传 Documents the Xinchuang (State-Informatization) Blind-Replacement Slowdown and the “Business-First Value-Based Rollout” Reframe, Positioning Huawei as the Sole Beneficiary of Xinchuang Stratification#

1. 国产信创外传 (Chinese State-Informatization Industry Outlet, WeChat) — “信创厂商自认盲目替换·告别野蛮替换时代” (Xinchuang Vendors Admit Blind Replacement — Farewell to the Barbaric-Replacement Era)

2. 国产信创外传 (WeChat) — “为什么越来越多单位暂停盲目国产化替换·真正的信创落地逻辑变了” (Why Increasingly More Units Are Pausing Blind Domestic-Localization Replacement — The Real Xinchuang Rollout Logic Has Changed)

3. Institutional claim — enterprises, government units, and energy-sector units have slowed one-size-fits-all Xinchuang replacement, retreating from replacement-rate-first KPIs toward business-first, risk-controlled, value-based rollout.

4. Institutional failure-mode diagnosis — the four Xinchuang-era problems: (i) 老旧业务系统适配工作量远超预估 (adaptation workload for legacy business systems far exceeds initial estimates); (ii) 上线后性能不达标 (post-deployment performance does not meet the initial benchmarks); (iii) 小众外设和工控软件缺少驱动 (niche peripheral devices and industrial-control software lack drivers); (iv) 后期运维成本飙升 (long-term operational-maintenance costs spike).

5. Institutional KPI reframe — from “replacement-coverage-rate” (替换覆盖率) to “business safety-driven deployment” (业务安全驱动); new three principles: 分级替代 (tiered replacement — office / management / production-core), 先评估后改造 (assess-before-modify), 灰度落地双轨验证 (gray-rollout dual-track verification); the operative frame is “能替则替、难替缓替” (replace-what-can-be-replaced, defer-what-cannot-be-replaced) — prioritizing business continuity over replacement-rate-maximization.

6. Institutional-risk taxonomy — the three risks being avoided by the pause: 业务中断风险 (business-interruption risk — critical systems in energy and finance cannot tolerate downtime), 隐性适配风险 (hidden-adaptation risk — legacy systems without source-code or vendor support), 长期运维风险 (long-term O&M risk — O&M teams lack the required platform-maintenance experience).

7. Institutional positioning claim — the piece explicitly states that “the goal of Xinchuang has never been to make everything domestic; the goal is critical-business autonomy and controllable risk” (信创的目标从来不是全部国产，而是关键业务自主可控风险可管控).

8. Institutional-industry-dynamics claim — “Huawei is the sole beneficiary in the Xinchuang stratification period; as the high-end vendor with its own chip design and process capability, Huawei is the only beneficiary in the stratification period; low-end markets are squeezed by high-end markets” (华为在信创分化期中是唯一受益的厂商，高端市场挤压低端市场).

The 国产信创外传’s two-piece publication is the first-documented instance in this series of the Xinchuang-blind-replacement-slowdown institutional object being operationalized at the state-informatization-industry-outlet-plus-value-based-rollout-plus-huawei-as-sole-beneficiary institutional-form layer simultaneously with this series’ October 1 briefing’s Higobase-Shandong-SASAC-Guantou-Jiehhe-5-year-exit-cycle-plus-1200-SOE-digital-technology-subsidiary-collapse layer and this series’ October 1 briefing’s open-source-label-as-market-positioning-vs-open-source-institutional-identity distinction layer — a two-surface-simultaneous Xinchuang-institutional-unwinding institutional-form observation.

The sharpest institutional-economics fact of the day: the 国产信创外传 piece’s own operational claim that “the goal of Xinchuang has never been to make everything domestic; the goal is critical-business autonomy and controllable risk” (信创的目标从来不是全部国产，而是关键业务自主可控风险可管控) — paired with the piece’s operational framing of “replace-what-can-be-replaced, defer-what-cannot-be-replaced” (能替则替、难替缓替) — is the sharpest documented retreat from the Xinchuang-era replacement-rate-first-KPI layer to the business-first-value-based-rollout layer. The retreat is not described as a policy change; the piece states that policy has not changed (政策方向没变). The retreat is described as an operational reframe — 从指标驱动转向业务安全驱动 (from KPI-driven to business-safety-driven) — occurring at the enterprise-deployment layer in response to the piece’s own four documented institutional failure modes. The piece’s institutional-positioning claim — “the goal of Xinchuang has never been to make everything domestic” — is a first-documented instance in this series of a state-informatization-industry outlet publicly reframing the operational objective of the same Xinchuang institutional object this series’ October 1 briefing documented at the Xinchuang-tail-mid-tier-domestic-replacement-database-vendor-exit layer and this series’ October 1 briefing’s 1200-SOE-digital-technology-subsidiary-collapse layer — the state-informatization-industry-outlet is now publishing a value-based-rollout reframe at the Xinchuang-institutional-object layer, at the same institutional layer at which the state capital is being rotated away from the Xinchuang-tail-mid-tier vendor toward the frontier-AI-consolidation-head.

The three-Xinchuang-era-failure-mode layer. Per the piece: “老旧业务系统适配工作量远超预估，上线后性能不达标，小众外设和工控软件缺少驱动，业务流程卡壳，一次性全量割接风险高，一旦故障直接影响核心生产，后期运维成本飙升，团队缺乏国产平台运维经验” (Legacy business-system adaptation workload far exceeds initial estimates; post-deployment performance does not meet initial benchmarks; niche peripherals and industrial-control software lack drivers; business flows are stalled; one-shot full-scale cutover carries high risk, and a failure directly affects core production; long-term O&M costs spike; O&M teams lack domestic-platform operational-maintenance experience). The institutional reading is that the three-Xinchuang-era-failure-mode layer is being operationalized at the state-informatization-industry-outlet-plus-value-based-rollout institutional-form layer — the piece is not just reporting a slowdown; it is publishing a specific four-item failure-mode diagnosis that pairs an operational-technical diagnosis (adaptation workload, performance, driver coverage) with an institutional-structural diagnosis (one-shot cutover risk, long-term O&M cost, team experience gap). This is a structurally distinct institutional-form object from the Western-market-competitive-positioning diagnostic layer.

The business-first-three-principles layer. Per the piece: (i) 分级替代 (tiered replacement — non-core office systems, management systems, and production-core business systems are treated as three distinct tiers); (ii) 先评估后改造 (assess-before-modify — no hardware procurement before a full current-state inventory, system-dependency mapping, peripheral-peak-pressure assessment, and modification-workload-and-risk estimation); (iii) 灰度落地双轨验证 (gray-rollout dual-track verification — simulation-environment testing first, then dual-track parallel with gray cutover, with a complete rollback plan). The institutional reading is that the business-first-three-principles layer is being operationalized at the state-informatization-industry-outlet-plus-value-based-rollout institutional-form layer — a structurally distinct institutional-form object from the state-directed-industrial-policy-instrument layer this series’ September 14 briefing documented. The Xinchuang-institutional-object is being reframed at the state-informatization-industry-outlet layer as a business-first, risk-controlled value-based-rollout instrument rather than a state-directed-replacement-rate-instrument.

The “Huawei-as-sole-beneficiary-in-Xinchuang-stratification-period” layer. Per the piece: “华为在信创分化期中是唯一受益的厂商，高端市场挤压低端市场，华为作为高端厂商自有工艺厂商是分化期中唯一受益者” (Huawei is the sole beneficiary in the Xinchuang stratification period; high-end markets squeeze low-end markets; Huawei, as a high-end vendor with its own chip design and process capability, is the sole beneficiary in the stratification period).
