4. Structural composition — three participant categories plus five directions plus single-application form.

  • (a) Three participant categories — open-source projects (开源项目), open-source industrial applications (产业应用), and enterprise practices (企业实践). The three-category taxonomy places the recognition call at a three-surface-simultaneous institutional-form layer — open-source-project-plus-industrial-application-plus-enterprise-practice — a category design that does not distinguish between software project, industry deployment, and enterprise internal practice.
  • (b) Five directions of peak practice — five typical practice directions covering the core links of open-source technology innovation and industrial application (the notice does not name the five directions in the excerpt, but the taxonomy is documented as five).
  • (c) Single-application form — each participant unit submits one application per practice, with a single submission form (报名内容单一化).

  • Application period — 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-25 (a 14-day window).
  • Screening period — 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-27 (a two-day formality-and-expert-review window).
