Institutional Change — CAICT Policy-and-Economics Institute Publishes the First Three-Layer Chinese Administrative Agent-Governance Framework#

1. CAICT Policy-and-Economics Institute + China University of Political Science and Law AI Law Research Institute WeChat — Agent Governance Research Report 2026 (37,848 characters / 105 lines)

The China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT) Policy-and-Economics Institute, in joint research with the AI Law Research Institute of China University of Political Science and Law, published on 2026-09-15 an agent governance research report — the first-documented Chinese-administrative-institute three-layer agent-governance framework this series’ prior twenty-seven briefings have documented. The CAICT is the Ministry-of-Industry-and-Information-Technology-affiliated research institute this series’ prior briefings have documented at the AI-administrative-standard surface (September 14 briefing: MIIT “AI + Software” Special Action Implementation Plan) and the AI-enterprise-support surface (September 6 briefing: MIIT AI SME Startup Support Plan 31号); the CAICT Agent Governance Research Report is the same administrative-institute surface being operationalized at the governance-framework surface — a structurally new institutional-form observation.

The report’s three-layer governance framework, verbatim from the CAICT text, is Foundation layer — data-and-identity base / Operation layer — execution-and-dynamic-control / Ecosystem layer — value-collaboration — the first-documented three-layer Chinese-administrative agent-governance framework at the CAICT-institute-report surface. The report’s operational diagnostic, verbatim from the CAICT text, is that “agents have leapt from dialog box to agent, but long-horizon complex-task capability still shows obvious shortcomings” — the first-documented CAICT-administrative-institute diagnostic placing the agent-surface at the long-horizon-task-capability-shortcoming layer, alongside this series’ September 14 briefing’s documentation of the MIIT “AI + Software” Special Action Implementation Plan’s Agent Software Application Store + Skills Resource Library placement at the same agent surface.

The report’s sharpest institutional-economics claim, verbatim from the CAICT text, is the report’s own reading of the agent-surface value-elevation triple: “traffic entry · user lock-in · commercial closed-loop three-tier value elevation” — the first-documented CAICT-administrative-institute reading of the agent-surface at the traffic-entry, user-lock-in, and commercial-closed-loop three-tier-value layer, pairing institutionally with this series’ September 15 briefing’s documentation of the Google Cloud Harness engineering surface (Tang Xin keynote at GOSIM Shenzhen 2026) at the same value-elevation layer.

The report’s four-surface-global-differentiated-governance diagnostic, verbatim from the CAICT text, places four jurisdictional governance surfaces at a single Chinese-administrative-institute-report surface:

  • US — frontier-capability diffusion prevention
  • EU — fundamental-rights protection
  • Singapore — global-trustworthy-AI hub
  • China — large-model institutional-stacking governance

The US / EU / Singapore / China four-surface pairing at the CAICT-administrative-institute-report surface is the first-documented instance of a Chinese-administrative-institute placing all four jurisdictional governance surfaces at a single institute-report surface. The report’s operational diagnostic of the Chinese surface, verbatim from the CAICT text, is that “China responds to AI+ application demand by publishing dedicated agent institutions, establishing safety bottom-lines, and deploying the agent-interconnect ecosystem with standards-first, exploring large-model institutional-stacking governance.” This is the first-documented CAICT-administrative-institute diagnostic placing the “large-model institutional-stacking governance” object at the same administrative-institute surface this series’ prior briefings have documented at the MIIT-industrial-policy layer and the CAICT-AI-administrative-standard layer.

Institutional significance: CAICT’s Agent Governance Research Report 2026 is the first-documented Chinese-administrative-institute three-layer agent-governance framework in this series, and it is being operationalized at four institutional surfaces simultaneously — the three-layer-governance-framework layer, the long-horizon-task-capability-shortcoming diagnostic layer, the traffic-entry / user-lock-in / commercial-closed-loop value-elevation layer, and the US / EU / Singapore / China global-differentiated-governance comparison layer — a four-surface-simultaneous-Chinese-administrative-agent-governance pattern.

Commentary#

Four institutionally new placements on the same unsolved institutional object that this series’ prior twenty-seven briefings have been documenting: the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders.

This cycle’s briefing documents a structurally new institutional pattern: the same Chinese open-source surface is being operationalized simultaneously at four institutionally distinct surfaces — the CAICT-administrative-institute three-layer agent-governance-framework surface (a 37,848-character governance-framework report jointly published by the CAICT Policy-and-Economics Institute and the CUPL AI Law Research Institute, placing US / EU / Singapore / China four-jurisdictional governance surfaces at a single Chinese-administrative-institute-report surface), the State-Council-formal-level Chinese-administrative-narrative surface (Jiang Xiaojuan’s 40,699-character 2026-Bund-Summit keynote placing the Chinese-open-source-model-overtook-US-since-June download-volume surface and the top-6-API-call-volume-ranking surface at the Bund-Summit institutional layer), the DeepSeek-internal-senior-technical-engineer personal-essay surface (Liu Shengyu’s 22,097-character personal essay placing the AI-operator technical-paradox diagnostic and the AI-learning-penalty data pairing at the personal-career-ethics layer), and the Huawei-supervisory-board-chair institutional-narrative surface (Guo Ping’s 55,430-character Voice-Community essay placing the “become NVIDIA” diagnostic and the R&D-above-revenue-plus-negative-operating-cash-flow financial-structure pairing at the supervisory-board-chair institutional-narrative layer).

The four placements are institutionally coherent on the same unsolved institutional object — the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders — and the four placements together document a four-surface-simultaneous-institutionalization pattern alongside the six-surface-simultaneous-institutionalization pattern this series’ September 16 briefing documented at the Chinese vendor-official cultural-definition layer, the A-share capital-market institutionalization layer for RISC-V CPU IP, the Chinese-vendor-official quantitative-evidence layer for Agent-Infra, and the Chinese-vendor-official analytical-reading layer.
