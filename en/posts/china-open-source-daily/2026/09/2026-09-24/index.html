⚠️ Editorial note: The open source ecosystem in China operates under a distinct institutional framework — characterized by state-led initiatives, intranet-like boundaries, and top-down governance. Readers should be aware that this context differs from the community-driven open source model common in other regions. Institutional Change — DeepSeek and Moonshot Invited to Brief the UN Security Council on AI: The First-Documented Instance of Chinese Frontier Labs Speaking at a US-Led International Governance Forum#

1. Reuters (2026-09-22) — DeepSeek + Moonshot invited to UN Security Council AI briefing · Sam Altman (OpenAI) + Anthropic senior rep co-briefing · DeepSeek founder Liang Wenfeng declined attendance · Chinese state media characterizing US-side AI slowdown calls as a “Cold War playbook” · US-China AI safety hotline talks continuing under Bessent’s Shenzhen follow-up

On September 22, 2026, Reuters reported that Chinese AI startup DeepSeek will be among the companies briefing the United Nations Security Council this week on the risks posed by artificial intelligence, weeks after several AI industry leaders called for a coordinated slowdown of the increasingly powerful AI systems, warning that they could soon improve on their own and slip beyond human control. Per Reuters’ reporting, the 15-member Security Council is set to meet on Wednesday to discuss AI and international security, with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman planning to brief the meeting, and “senior representatives of Anthropic” also participating. Crucially, “DeepSeek and other Chinese AI firms including Moonshot have been invited to make statements.”

The first-documented instance in this series of a Chinese frontier AI lab being invited to brief the United Nations Security Council on AI risk alongside US frontier-lab leaders is the sharpest institutional-economics fact of the day. The UN Security Council is the primary forum of the US-led international-security apparatus; the invitation of DeepSeek — a Chinese state-invested-for-profit open-weight frontier model lab — to speak at the same Wednesday briefings as OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Anthropic senior representatives is a first-documented instance of this series. The invitation extends beyond DeepSeek to include Moonshot (月之暗面) — the Chinese frontier-AI-lab this series’ September 23 briefing documented at the Kimi K3 open-weight-license-plus-commercial-monetization-threshold layer, the confidential-HK-IPO-filing-plus-three-global-underwriting-advisors layer, and the Microsoft-Amazon-Google-cloud-hosting-plus-30-percent-revenue-share layer — and to other Chinese AI firms. From an institutional economics standpoint, this UN-Security-Council-AI-briefing / Chinese-frontier-lab-invitation pairing is a structural finding: the UN Security Council apparatus this series’ prior briefings have not documented at the Chinese-frontier-AI-lab-invitation layer is being operationalized at the US-led-international-governance-forum-invitation layer simultaneously.

The sharpest institutional-economics object of the day: the Liang Wenfeng physical absence. Per Reuters’ reporting, DeepSeek founder Liang Wenfeng (梁文锋) — the same founder this series’ September 12 briefing documented at the Reuters Chinese-AI-IPO-comparison-to-Moonshot-layer, and this series’ prior briefings have documented at the DeepSeek-mid-2025 open-weight-frontier-moment layer — does not plan to attend. Reuters further documents: “Unlike leaders of US frontier labs such as OpenAI and Anthropic, the heads of China’s leading model developers have rarely publicly articulated the possibility that increasingly capable AI systems themselves could deceive humans, evade control or cause catastrophic harm. They have also remained largely silent on those risks since the debate intensified in the US this month.” This frontier-lab-leader-physical-absence / US-led-international-governance-forum-invitation pairing is institutionally distinctive: DeepSeek has been invited to the same forum as OpenAI’s CEO and Anthropic’s senior rep, but DeepSeek’s founder is declining attendance — the same DeepSeek entity this series’ prior briefings have documented at the open-weight-frontier-moment layer is now being documented at the UN-Security-Council-AI-briefing-invitation layer with a founder-physical-absence. This is a first-documented instance in this series of a Chinese-frontier-lab-founder-physical-absence-at-a-US-led-international-governance-forum that has invited the Chinese frontier lab to brief.

The state-media framing of US-side slowdown calls. Reuters further documents that Chinese state media have “dismissed calls for a slowdown in AI development as stemming from a ‘Cold War playbook,’ aimed at preserving Washington’s dominance in cutting-edge technology and excluding Beijing from the global AI governance system.” This Chinese-state-media-Cold-War-playbook framing of US-side AI-slowdown calls pairs institutionally with this series’ September 12 briefing’s documentation of DeepSeek’s hiring of Yan Wentao (formerly of the CCP Central Committee’s Foreign Affairs Commission) as its first CFO ahead of a possible STAR Market IPO — the same DeepSeek entity this cycle’s briefing documents at the UN-Security-Council-AI-briefing-invitation layer with a founder-physical-absence is institutionally embedded in the state-directed-capital-and-central-committee-connected layer simultaneously.

The Bessent AI safety hotline follow-up. Reuters documents that “Senior US and Chinese officials agreed to hold further talks on AI safety, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Monday, after meeting ahead of Trump’s scheduled talks with Chinese president Xi Jinping later this week in Washington.” This US-Treasury-Secretary-Bessent-plus-China-side AI-safety-hotline-follow-up is the first-documented instance in this series of a US-China bilateral AI-safety-hotline follow-up operationalized at the Treasury-Secretary-Speech layer simultaneously with the UN-Security-Council-AI-briefing layer. The Shenzhen follow-up (per Reuters’ linked 2026-09-21 reporting) is an institutionally distinctive bilateral channel: it is being operationalized at the Treasury-Secretary-plus-China-side channel layer alongside the UN-Security-Council AI-briefing channel layer.

Institutional significance: DeepSeek’s invitation to brief the UN Security Council on AI is the first-documented instance of a Chinese frontier AI lab being invited to speak at a US-led international governance forum on AI risk in this series, and it is being operationalized at four institutional surfaces simultaneously — the UN-Security-Council-AI-briefing-invitation layer, the Liang-Wenfeng-physical-absence-at-US-led-forum layer, the Chinese-state-media-Cold-War-playbook-framing-of-US-side-slowdown-calls layer, and the Bessent-China-side AI-safety-hotline-follow-up layer — a four-surface-simultaneous US-led-international-governance-forum-plus-Chinese-frontier-lab-invitation pattern.

Institutional Change — Dual Interpretations of the MIIT “AI + Software” Special Action Implementation Plan: The First-Documented Instance of a MIIT Central-Administrative Plan Being Operationalized at Two Institutionally Distinct State-Affiliated Interpretation Layers Simultaneously#

3. Liu Xiangan (刘贤刚), President of the National Institute of Standards and Technology for Information Technology (电子标准院), and Wang Zhiqin (王志勤), Vice President of CAICT — Two WeChat essays published within the same 24-hour window (September 22–23, 2026) — Liu’s interpretation at the electronic-standards-institute layer · Wang’s interpretation at the MIIT-affiliated-research-institute layer

The MIIT “AI + Software” Special Action Implementation Plan (《人工智能+软件专项行动实施方案》) — the MIIT central administrative plan this series’ September 14 briefing documented at the MIIT-Central-Administrative-Plan layer, and this series’ September 21 briefing’s MIIT-deputy-minister opening-remarks layer — has been interpreted within the same 24-hour window by two state-affiliated institutional surfaces operating at different institutional levels.

The Liu Xiangan interpretation (September 22–23, 2026). Liu Xiangan (刘贤刚), President of the National Institute of Standards and Technology for Information Technology (National Institute of Electronics Standards / 电子标准院), published a 4,123-character essay titled “我国软件产业’由大到强’亟需人工智能深度赋能丨《‘人工智能+软件’专项行动实施方案》解读” (Our Country’s Software Industry’s ‘From Large to Strong’ Urgently Needs Deep AI Empowerment | Interpretation of the “AI + Software” Special Action Implementation Plan). Per the raw input summary, Liu’s interpretation documents:

  • 2025 software business revenue: 15.48 trillion RMB (¥15.48万亿), which is 6.2× 2012 revenue with a 15.1% annual compound growth rate
  • The plan’s explicit 2028 and 2030 development targets
  • 6 aspects, 19 tasks (6个方面19项任务)
  • “By 2028, coverage of 20,000 large-and-above-scale software enterprises” (2万家规模以上软件企业), with 100 cumulative software-enterprise intelligent-transformation projects
  • “By 2028, incubate 5 or more high-quality open source projects” (孵化5个以上优质开源项目), relying on the nationwide open-source foundations and AI open-source communities to guide enterprises to open their outcomes
  • “Promote the application of the world’s first open-source license, recognized internationally by OSI, in Chinese-English bilingual — the Mulan Soft License” (推广全球首个获OSI国际认可的中英文双语木兰宽松开源许可证) — the OSI-recognized bilingual Mulan Soft License is being operationalized at the MIIT-Central-Administrative-Plan layer explicitly for promotion
  • SBOM standards development (软件物料清单SBOM标准研制), agent interface key standard development, agent identity-label and behavior-control standards (智能体身份标识与行为管控), open-source project maturity assessment, intelligent-programming capability maturity-tiered assessment, and a “intelligent-native open-source identifier and identity service network interoperable with international standards” (智能原生可衔接国际标准的智能体标识与身份服务网络)
  • The AI-plus-Small-and-Medium-Enterprise startup support plan and the Small-and-Medium-Enterprise open-source startup partner action

The Liu-Xiangan-President-of-the-Electronic-Standards-Institute / OSI-recognized-Mulan-Soft-License-explicit-promotion / SBOM-and-agent-identity-standards layer pairing is a structural finding: the same MIIT central administrative plan this series’ September 14 briefing documented at the MIIT Central-Administrative-Plan layer is now being operationalized at the electronic-standards-institute-plus-agent-identity-standards-plus-SBOM-standards-plus-open-source-maturity-assessment-plus-OSI-recognized-bilingual-license-explicit-promotion layer simultaneously. This is the first-documented instance in this series of a MIIT central administrative plan being operationalized at the OSI-recognized-bilingual-license-explicit-promotion layer.

The Wang Zhiqin interpretation (September 22–23, 2026). Wang Zhiqin (王志勤), Vice President of the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT), published a 4,536-character essay titled “推动’人工智能+软件’深度融合，赋能软件产业高质量发展” (Promoting the Deep Integration of ‘AI + Software’ to Empower High-Quality Development of the Software Industry). Per the raw input summary, Wang’s interpretation documents:

  • The “four principles” (四原则): application-driven (应用牵引), innovation-driven (创新驱动), safe-and-controllable (安全可控), ecosystem-coordinated (生态协同)
  • Two-stage plan for 2028 and 2030 (两步走)
  • CAICT 2026 survey data: “Enterprise AI code generation adoption rate exceeds 40%” (企业AI代码生成采纳率超过40%); “Nearly 30% of enterprises have intelligent development tool penetration reaching 90%” (近三成企业智能化开发工具普及率已达90%)
  • The four-agent-layered genealogy: general-purpose agents, vertical-domain agents, professional agents, industrial agents (智能体培育 通用/终端/垂直领域/工业 四层谱系)
  • Four policy instruments: open-competition-and-selection (揭榜挂帅), fiscal-special-funds, first-version-software-product-recognition (首版次软件产品认定), and the “government-helping-horse-on-the-horse-and-seeing-it-one-journey” (政府扶上马送一程) framing — the first-documented state-directed industrial-policy phrase
  • The dual-drive framework: “government + market” (双轮驱动)
  • Open-source instruments: “relying on nationwide open-source foundations as the carrier, through first-launch-support mechanisms, sector-area construction, and other measures” (以全国性开源基金会为载体 通过首发支持机制 领域专区建设 等方式)

The Wang-Zhiqin-CAICT-Vice-President / four-principles-plus-four-agent-layered-genealogy-plus-four-policy-instruments-plus-CAICT-2026-survey-40-percent-adoption-rate / first-launch-support-mechanism-and-sector-area-construction layer pairing is a structural finding: the same MIIT central administrative plan is now being operationalized at the CAICT-Vice-President-plus-CAICT-2026-survey-plus-four-policy-instrument-plus-open-source-foundation-as-carrier layer simultaneously. This is a first-documented instance in this series of a MIIT central administrative plan being operationalized at the CAICT-Vice-President-plus-CAICT-2026-survey-40-percent-adoption-rate layer simultaneously with the Electronic-Standards-Institute-plus-OSI-recognized-bilingual-license-explicit-promotion layer — a dual-state-affiliated-interpretation-layer operationalization of the same MIIT central administrative plan.

Wang Zhiqin’s four-policy-instrument apparatus (open-competition-and-selection / fiscal-special-funds / first-version-software-product-recognition / government-helping-horse-on-the-horse-and-seeing-it-one-journey) pairs institutionally with this series’ September 2 briefing’s documentation of Chongqing’s 15th-Five-Year-Plan AI framework’s open-source-infrastructure-and-compliance codification at the municipal-plan layer, and this series’ September 10 briefing’s documentation of the Shanghai Open Source Industry Investment Fund as a municipal-scale capital-instrument object. Institutional Change — openKylin Joins the National Industrial Cybersecurity Development and Research Center as a Special-Donation Unit: The First-Documented Instance of an Open-Source OS Foundation Being Operationalized at a State-Affiliated-Cybersecurity-Center-Steward Layer#

4. openKylin WeChat (2026-09-22) — 国家工业信息安全发展研究中心暨常务委员会9月会议 · OpenAtom Foundation openKylin community Special-Donation-Unit · T/SIA 056-2025 Controllable-Open-Source-OS-System Group Standard · 薪火杯 Leading-Application Case Collection · Community Graduation Process · Constant-Unit Contribution Strengthening Mechanism · openKylin 3.0 Kernel Jump to Intelligent-Native · SCO (Shanghai Cooperation Organization) openKylin Achievement List

On September 22, 2026, the OpenAtom Foundation’s openKylin community held its September steering-committee meeting at the National Industrial Cybersecurity Development and Research Center (NICS / 国家工业信息安全发展研究中心). Per the openKylin community organizing report, the National Industrial Cybersecurity Development and Research Center participated “in the identity of openKylin community Special-Donation Unit” (以openKylin社区特别捐赠单位身份), long-term deeply cultivating the community’s ecosystem construction and fulfilling the responsibilities of Steering-Committee Member Units while cooperating to carry out community governance.

The National-Industrial-Cybersecurity-Development-and-Research-Center / openKylin-community-Special-Donation-Unit layer pairing is a structural finding: this is the first-documented instance in this series of an open-source-operating-system-community being operationalized at a state-affiliated-cybersecurity-center-steward layer. NICS is a state-affiliated cybersecurity research institute; the openKylin community is an open-source operating system community under the OpenAtom Foundation; and the NICS-as-Special-Donation-Unit pairing places the NICS state-affiliated-cybersecurity-research-institute surface at the open-source-operating-system-community steward layer.

The T/SIA 056-2025 Controllable-Open-Source-OS-System Group Standard. Per the organizing report, “In the construction of open-source standardization, the National Industrial Cybersecurity Development and Research Center deeply participated in drafting the T/SIA 056-2025《开源操作系统社区可控开源体系建设规范》(Open-Source Operating System Community Controllable-Open-Source-System Construction Specification) group standard led by the community, assisting in the normalized construction of the open-source system.”

The T/SIA 056-2025 可控开源体系 (Controllable-Open-Source-System) group standard is the sharpest institutional-economics object of the day: this is a first-documented instance in this series of a Chinese group standard explicitly codifying the term “controllable open source system” (可控开源体系) at the open-source-operating-system-community layer. The term 可控 (controllable) is being codified as a group-standard layer object at the open-source-OS-community layer. From an institutional economics standpoint, this is the first-documented group-standard-plus-controllable-open-source-system codification layer pairing in this series, and it pairs institutionally with this series’ September 19 briefing’s documentation of the SJ/T 12304-2026 two-axis-67-indicator LLM open-source-grading industry-standard layer, this series’ September 2 briefing’s documentation of Chongqing’s 15th-Five-Year-Plan AI framework’s open-source-infrastructure-and-compliance codification at the municipal-plan layer, and this cycle’s briefing’s documentation of Liu Xiangan’s SBOM-standards-and-agent-identity-standards-and-open-source-maturity-assessment layer at the Electronic-Standards-Institute layer — the same Chinese-administrative grading-and-maturity-instrument surface this series’ prior briefings have documented at multiple layers is now being operationalized at the group-standard-plus-controllable-open-source-system layer simultaneously.

The open-source community graduation process. Per the organizing report, the steering committee’s open agenda focused on “improving the community graduation process” (社区毕业流程完善) and “strengthening the constant-unit contribution mechanism” (常委单位贡献强化机制). The community-graduation-process-plus-constant-unit-contribution-strengthening mechanism is the first-documented instance in this series of an open-source-operating-system-community operationalizing a graduation-process-plus-contribution-strengthening-mechanism at the OpenAtom-Foundation-plus-NICS-steward layer.
