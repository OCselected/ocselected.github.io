Three institutionally dense placements on the same unsolved institutional object this series' prior thirty-six briefings have been documenting — the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders. (1) WeChat 早安胡同 (2026-09-24): 统信软件 (Tongxin / UOS) and 银河麒麟软件 (Kylin) — the two leading desktop operating system vendors in the China Center for Information Security (CCIC) 安全可靠测评准入制 (Security-and-Reliability Assessment Access) regime — have both brought the 方德鸳鸯火锅 (Fangde Uanyang / 方德鸳鸯火锅 V8.0) compatibility platform into their respective application stores, with 100+ industrial-software and creative-design-specific software titles passing compatibility testing for one-click download from the app stores, and the platform based on a deep modification of open-source Wine to address legacy-industrial-software and hardware-peripheral compatibility. Institutional Change — Tongxin (UOS) and Kylin Both Integrate Fangde Uanyang (Wine-based) Windows Compatibility Layer: The First-Documented Operationalization of the Jiashu-Investment Chairman Consolidation Move at a Joint-Product-Milestone Layer and the First-Documented Operationalization of the Two-Routes Xinchuang-Desktop-OS Framework#

1. WeChat 早安胡同 (2026-09-24) — 两大头部OS都接入方德鸳鸯火锅, Windows兼容要统一标准了 (Two of the Top Two OS Vendors Both Integrate Fangde Uanyang, Windows Compatibility Needs a Unified Standard) · 统信UOS V20 + 麒麟V10 with 方德鸳鸯火锅 V8.0 deep mutual-recognition · 100+ industrial-software and creative-design-specific software titles pass compatibility testing · Based on open-source Wine deep modification · One-click download from OS app stores · Xinchuang-industry specialization transition · Compatibility-school 守存量 vs. Full-stack-school 攻增量 two-routes framework

On September 24, 2026, the 早安胡同 (Good Morning Hutong) WeChat account published a commentary reporting that 统信软件 (Tongxin / UOS) and 银河麒麟软件 (Kylin Software) — the two top Chinese desktop operating-system vendors in the CCIC 安全可靠测评准入制 (China Center for Information Security Security-and-Reliability Assessment Access Regime) — have both brought 方德鸳鸯火锅 (Fangde Uanyang / 方德鸳鸯火锅 V8.0) into their respective application stores with 深度互认 (deep mutual-recognition) integration. Per the source’s reporting:

  • Tongxin UOS V20 + Kylin V10 both completed deep mutual-recognition integration with 方德鸳鸯火锅 V8.0.
  • 100+ industrial-software and creative-design-specific software titles completed compatibility testing; end users can now download these titles one-click from either OS’s application store.
  • 方德鸳鸯火锅 (Fangde Uanyang) is a commercial compatibility platform based on a deep modification of open-source Wine, specifically engineered to solve the compatibility problem for legacy industrial software and hardware peripherals.
  • The pre-integration state: previously 统信 (Tongxin) and 麒麟 (Kylin) had each been developing their own Windows-compatibility solutions, resulting in version fragmentation and inconsistent user experience across the two top OS vendors in the same market.
  • The post-integration state: both OS vendors have unified on the same compatibility layer, achieving Xinchuang-industry division of labor (信创产业分工协作) — OS vendors focus on the OS itself (Xinchuang certification, security hardening, channel coverage), and the compatibility-layer vendor focuses on Windows-ecosystem migration and the hard problems of industrial software and legacy-peripheral plug-ins.
  • The two-routes framework: the source frames the post-consolidation Xinchuang desktop-OS competitive structure as a clear two-routes division of labor — Compatibility-school (统信 + 麒麟 + 方德) 守存量 (retaining the stock) — a smooth-migration route that defends the existing Windows-based installed base by providing a compatibility layer for legacy industrial software and peripherals — versus Full-stack-school (Huawei HarmonyOS) 攻增量 (attacking the increment) — a multi-endpoint-synergy route that targets new deployments and future markets through Huawei’s HarmonyOS ecosystem.

The sharpest institutional-economics object of the day: the joint-product-milestone operationalization of the Jiashu-Investment chairman consolidation. This series’ September 9 briefing documented that Jiashu Investment (嘉树投资), a private-equity firm, achieved chairman control of both Tongxin (UOS) and Fangde (中科方德 / NewStartCG) through a two-step Fangde acquisition between August 27 and September 2, 2026 — capital injection from 0 to 12.01% followed by old-share acquisition from 12.01% to 30.51%, with Jiashu’s founder Lin Wei simultaneously chairman of both Tongxin and Fangde. The September 11 briefing’s Xinchuang-Lutoushe half-year scorecard documented the parallel Huawei-horizontal-consolidation observation at the horizontal-full-stack layer. This cycle’s briefing documents the first-documented operationalization of the Jiashu-Investment chairman consolidation move at a joint-product-milestone-and-division-of-labor layer — the same chairman position this series’ September 9 briefing documented at the private-equity-chairman-consolidation surface is now being operationalized at a joint-product-milestone layer, with Tongxin and Fangde jointly publishing a Windows-compatibility product milestone at the UOS+Kylin-2-top-vendors-plus-Fangde-3-routes Xinchuang-desktop-OS surface. The Tongxin-Fangde chairman-consolidation-plus-joint-product-milestone pairing is a structural finding: the same desktop-OS consolidation logic this series’ September 9 briefing documented at the private-equity-chairman-consolidation layer is now being operationalized at the joint-product-milestone layer, and the joint-product-milestone layer places a specific product object — a Wine-based Windows compatibility platform — at the surface of the consolidation outcome.

The sharpest popular-commentary-layer formalization: the Xinchuang-industry specialization transition from redundant-invention to division-of-labor. Per the source’s framing, the Xinchuang-industry pattern before this integration was 各自为战造轮子 (each-fighting-on-their-own-and-re-inventing-the-wheel) with 版本混乱用户体验不一致 (version-fragmentation and inconsistent user experience), and the pattern after this integration is 分工协作提体验 (division-of-labor-and-user-experience-elevation) — with the specialization axis being OS-vendor (OS-certification-plus-security-hardening-plus-channel-coverage) versus compatibility-layer-vendor (Windows-ecosystem-migration-plus-industrial-software-plus-legacy-peripheral-hard-problems). This is the first-documented operationalization of the domestic-OS-industry-specialization-transition-from-redundant-invention-to-division-of-labor at the WeChat-monitor-popular-commentary layer, pairing with this series’ September 12 briefing’s documentation of the same 国产系统是"制度产品"不是"技术产品" (domestic systems are institutional products, not technical products) framing at the WeChat-monitor-popular-commentary layer — the same domestic-OS-as-institutional-product framing this series’ September 12 briefing documented at the WeChat-monitor-popular-commentary layer is now being operationalized at the domestic-OS-industry-specialization-transition layer simultaneously.

The sharpest two-routes-framework operationalization: compatibility-school 守存量 vs. full-stack-school 攻增量. The source frames the post-consolidation Xinchuang desktop-OS competitive structure as two institutionally distinct routes — Compatibility-school (统信 + 麒麟 + 方德) 守存量 (retaining the stock) versus Full-stack-school (Huawei HarmonyOS) 攻增量 (attacking the increment). The 兼容派 (compatibility school) is composed of the two Jiashu-Investment-connected top OS vendors (统信) plus the third desktop-OS vendor (麒麟) plus the Fangde compatibility layer; the 全栈派 (full-stack school) is Huawei HarmonyOS, the state-enterprise-ecosystem-entering vendor this series’ September 11 briefing documented at the HarmonyOS-national-test-pass plus future-erosion-of-Kirin-market Xinchuang-Lutoushe scorecard layer. Institutional significance: The 统信-麒麟-方德鸳鸯火锅 joint-product-milestone is the first-documented operationalization of the Jiashu-Investment chairman consolidation move (this series’ September 9 briefing) at a joint-product-milestone-and-division-of-labor layer in this series, and it is being operationalized at four institutional surfaces simultaneously — the joint-product-milestone layer, the domestic-OS-industry-specialization-transition-from-redundant-invention-to-division-of-labor layer, the two-routes Xinchuang-desktop-OS framework (compatibility-school vs. full-stack-school) layer, and the WeChat-monitor-popular-commentary layer — a four-surface-simultaneous domestic-OS-layer-institutionalization pattern.

  • Three-module curriculum: (a) open-source-overview-and-enterprise-value, (b) enterprise-intranet-governance-plus-open-source-governance-system, (c) enterprise-participation-plus-open-source-project-creation.
