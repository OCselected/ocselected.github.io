Institutional Change — Xi Jinping’s BRICS-Plus Open-Source AI Initiative at the 18th BRICS Summit#

1. Chinese Foreign Ministry / Xinhua / Reuters / CNBC / Euronews / New Indian Express / CGTN / The Daily Star / AFP (September 12–14, 2026) — Xi proposes five-point initiative for ‘greater BRICS’ cooperation, centered on a China-led ‘open-source zone for artificial intelligence’

On September 13, 2026 — the second day of the 18th BRICS Summit held at the Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, India, chaired by India — Chinese President Xi Jinping delivered an address proposing a five-point initiative for ‘greater BRICS’ cooperation. The proposal was carried simultaneously by the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs’ readout, Xinhua’s wire, CGTN’s coverage, and by Reuters / CNBC / Euronews / New Indian Express / The Daily Star / AFP international wire services.

The five-point initiative, verbatim from the Chinese Foreign Ministry readout, is:

  • Initiative 1 (open-source and inclusive AI): “China will be a pioneer in establishing a BRICS AI open source community, support the cooperation in developing and applying large language models, hold specialised AI seminars and training courses, and build an open AI ecosystem.”
  • Initiative 2 (trade and investment facilitation): A BRICS Special Economic Zone partnership.
  • Initiative 3 (digital industry): Establishment of a “BRICS digital ecosystem cloud platform” and a “digital ecosystem cloud platform” for reducing the costs and technical barriers for businesses adopting AI.
  • Initiative 4 (intelligent manufacturing): Cooperation on smart manufacturing and industrial supply chains.
  • Initiative 5 (science and technology talent development): Establishment of a BRICS engineer-cultivation alliance and a BRICS youth exchange program for scientific and technological innovation.

The sharpest institutional-economics fact in the initiative is Initiative 1’s naming of a ‘BRICS AI open source community’ as a China-led project for the 11-member BRICS grouping (Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa, Saudi Arabia, Iran, Indonesia, Ethiopia, Egypt, and the United Arab Emirates). This is the first-documented instance of a Chinese-head-of-state placing a ‘BRICS open-source AI zone’ object at the BRICS Summit institutional layer — the same Global-South open-source instrument this series’ September 2 briefing documented at the COPU / WAICO-50-vs-2 layer is being operationalized at the BRICS-plus institutional layer simultaneously.

The initiative’s four institutional pairings, verbatim from the Foreign Ministry readout:

  • 5,000 training opportunities for developing countries — China’s pledge to “provide developing countries with 5,000 opportunities for AI training and seminars over the next five years” — a quantified training-pipeline pledge paired with the BRICS open-source AI community object.
  • 6 international AI application cooperation centers — to be established “with ASEAN, the League of Arab States, the African Union, the Community of Latin American and Caribbean States, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization and BRICS.”
  • China assuming the rotating BRICS chairmanship in 2027 — China will host the 19th BRICS Summit next year, giving Beijing direct host-side operational authority over the initiative’s Year-2 rollout.
  • China’s domestic 10-billion-download baseline — Xi’s July 17 WAIC keynote documented “China’s open-source large AI models have been downloaded more than 10 billion times in total,” which is the quantitative baseline that Initiative 1 is institutionalizing at the BRICS-plus layer.

The initiative’s sharpest institutional-economics global-trade framing, verbatim from the Xinhua readout, is Xi’s diagnostic that “greater BRICS” must “uphold openness and cooperation for mutual benefit and win-win outcomes,” “keep industrial and supply chains stable and unimpeded,” and “foster integrated markets” — the first-documented Chinese-head-of-state diagnostic placing the BRICS-plus open-source AI community object at the BRICS industrial-supply-chain-stability layer.

The initiative’s sharpest institutional-economics US-counter-offer diagnostic, verbatim from Reuters’ framing, is that China’s open-source AI commitment is being positioned as an “alternative to Washington” at the same BRICS Summit where US AI leaders have called for voluntary model-slowdown rules. From an institutional economics standpoint, this BRICS-open-source-AI-zone / US-voluntary-slowdown-model-counter-offer pairing is a structural finding: the same open-source AI institutional question is being formulated at two institutionally distinct surfaces simultaneously — the Chinese-head-of-state BRICS Summit layer (an affirmative China-led open-source AI initiative) and the US-head-of-state voluntary model-slowdown layer (a restraint-based AI governance proposal) — with the two institutional surfaces operating in directly counter-offering relationship in the same month.

Institutional significance: Xi Jinping’s BRICS-plus open-source AI initiative is the first-documented Chinese-head-of-state placement of a ‘BRICS open-source AI zone’ object at the BRICS Summit institutional layer in this series, and it is being operationalized at five institutional surfaces simultaneously — the BRICS AI open-source-community layer, the BRICS digital ecosystem cloud platform layer, the BRICS engineer-cultivation-alliance-and-youth-exchange layer, the international AI application cooperation centers layer, and the 2027 BRICS rotating chairmanship operational-authority layer — a five-surface-simultaneous Chinese-head-of-state BRICS-plus open-source institutionalization pattern.

Institutional Change — HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 Documents CANN’s Transition to Sustained Community-Driven Open-Source Development and Ascend’s Formal PyTorch Backend Status#

1. Huawei / Media OutReach Newswire / Newsfile (September 17, 2026) — Wang Tao · Advancing the Agentic World, Building a Solid Silicon Foundation (HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 Opening Keynote)

Huawei’s Deputy Chairman and Rotating Chairman Wang Tao delivered the opening keynote of HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 on September 17, 2026 in Shanghai — the first-documented instance of a Huawei-deputy-chairman placing the Huawei CANN + Ascend open-source ecosystem surface at the HUAWEI CONNECT institutional layer in this series. Wang Tao is the Huawei executive surface this series’ prior twenty-eight briefings have not documented at this scale; the HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 keynote is the same Huawei surface being operationalized at the deputy-chairman keynote layer — a structurally new institutional-form observation.

The keynote’s sharpest institutional-economics claim, verbatim from the Wang Tao text, is that “CANN has moved to sustained, community-driven open-source development.” This is the first-documented Huawei-deputy-chairman statement placing the CANN object at the community-driven open-source-development layer. The same keynote documents that “Ascend now spans over 90 leading third-party open-source projects and is officially supported as a PyTorch accelerator backend” — the first-documented Huawei-deputy-chairman statement placing the Ascend object at the PyTorch-official-backend layer. The institutional-economics significance of these two claims is that the same Huawei surface this series’ September 15 briefing documented at the GOSIM Shenzhen keynote layer (Huawei’s Ren Xudong on the Black-Light Community paradigm shift, and Huawei’s Ma Yongqiang as LLaMA-Factory committer on the Ascend open-source training ecosystem keynote) is being operationalized at the Huawei-deputy-chairman HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer simultaneously — a two-layer-simultaneous-Huawei open-source-training-ecosystem pattern.

The keynote’s second institutional-economics diagnostic, verbatim from the Wang Tao text, is the Ascend-960DT Q1-2027 launch acceleration — “Ascend 960DT will be available in Q1 2027, three quarters ahead of the company’s original roadmap. And the Ascend 960PR will be ready in Q3 2027, one quarter ahead of schedule.” This is the first-documented Huawei-deputy-chairman statement placing the Ascend-960DT object at the Q1-2027-acceleration layer. The keynote’s Ascend roadmap continuation, verbatim from the Wang Tao text, is that “In 2028 and 2029, we will roll out the Ascend 970 and 980 chips, respectively. Thanks to the Tau (τ) Scaling Law, not only will their compute specifications continue to double, but you can…” — the first-documented Huawei-deputy-chairman citation of the Tau Scaling Law as a Huawei-internal-computing-scaling object. The institutional-economics significance of the Q1-2027 acceleration is that the same DeepSeek-Ascend-950DT-order-object this series’ September 10 briefing documented at the DeepSeek-order-commitment and Huawei-output-capacity-gap layer is being operationalized at the Huawei-deputy-chairman accelerated-roadmap layer simultaneously — a two-layer-simultaneous-DeepSeek-order-commitment-and-Huawei-output-capacity-gap pattern.

The keynote’s third institutional-economics diagnostic, verbatim from the Wang Tao text, is the Atlas-960E-SuperPoD NPO technology — “Atlas 960E SuperPoD — the industry’s first to use NPO.” NPO (Near-Package Optics) is documented as an innovation that reduces round-trip latency to “as low as 2 microseconds,” enabling training of AI models with up to 10 trillion parameters. The Atlas-960E SuperPoD can scale to incorporate 4,096 cards. The keynote’s sharpest institutional-economics diagnostic on this surface is that Huawei’s “agentic SuperCluster can scale up to one million NPUs” when interconnected with UnifiedBus. This is the first-documented Huawei-deputy-chairman placement of a one-million-NPU-SuperCluster object at the HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer.

The keynote’s fourth institutional-economics diagnostic, verbatim from the Wang Tao text, is the 4.16-million-Kunpeng-developer ecosystem — “the Kunpeng ecosystem has attracted 4.16 million developers from around the world.” The 4.16-million-developer figure is the first-documented Huawei-deputy-chairman placement of a Kunpeng-ecosystem scale object at the HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer. The 4.16-million-Kunpeng-developer surface pairs with the 90-third-party-open-source-Ascend-projects surface and the Ascend-PyTorch-official-backend surface as the same Huawei-deputy-chairman ecosystem-scale surface.

The keynote’s sharpest institutional-economics commercial-model diagnostic, verbatim from the Wang Tao text, is that “Huawei’s AI strategy is centered on computing power, with a focus on monetizing hardware.” This is the first-documented Huawei-deputy-chairman diagnostic placing the “we want to be a shovel-seller” commercial-model object this series’ September 17 briefing documented at the Huawei Supervisory Board Chair Guo Ping’s Voice Community essay layer at the Huawei-deputy-chairman HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer simultaneously — a two-layer-simultaneous-Huawei shovel-seller-commercial-model pattern.

Institutional significance: Wang Tao’s HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 keynote is the first-documented Huawei-deputy-chairman keynote surface in this series, and it is being operationalized at six institutional surfaces simultaneously — the CANN community-driven open-source-development layer, the Ascend PyTorch official-backend layer, the Atlas-960E-NPO-near-package-optics layer, the Ascend-960DT-Q1-2027-acceleration-and-Ascend-960PR-Q3-2027-and-Ascend-970-980-2028-2029-Tau-Scaling-Law roadmap layer, the one-million-NPU-UnifiedBus-SuperCluster layer, and the 4.16-million-Kunpeng-developer-and-90-third-party-open-source-Ascend-projects ecosystem-scale layer — a six-surface-simultaneous Huawei-deputy-chairman institutional-narrative pattern.

Institutional Change — OpenAtom Foundation Launches Its First Donor-Only Open-Source General-Knowledge Training Program#

1. OpenAtom Foundation WeChat (September 17, 2026) — Open Source General-Knowledge Training Class Phase 1 · Enterprise Open Source Training (面向捐赠人的培训)

The OpenAtom Foundation (开放原子开源基金会), the Chinese government-backed open-source foundation this series’ August 19 briefing documented at the AtomGit national-level open-source-community designation layer and this series’ August 30 briefing documented at the Mulan Foundation layer, announced on September 17, 2026 the launch of the Open Source General-Knowledge Training Class Phase 1 (企业开源通识研修班第一期) — the first-documented OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program surface in this series.

The training program’s specific operational details, verbatim from the OpenAtom Foundation WeChat text:

  • Dates: September 23–24, 2026
  • Location: Beijing
  • Audience: Donors of the OpenAtom Foundation (捐赠人)
  • Structure: Three thematic modules — (1) Open Source Overview and Enterprise Value, (2) Enterprise Internal Open Source and Open Source Governance Systems, (3) Enterprise Participation and Open Source Project Creation
  • Format: Discussion + certification (研讨+证书双收获) — a training-and-certification dual-outcome structure
  • Opening remarks: OpenAtom Foundation Chairman Xie Shaofeng (谢少锋)
  • Featured keynote: Jiang Tao (蒋涛), CSDN Founder and OpenAtom Foundation Board Director, speaking on “Open Source in the AI Era: Ecosystem and Infrastructure”

The training program’s sharpest institutional-economics fact is that it is aimed exclusively at the Foundation’s donors (面向捐赠人的培训) — this is not an open-participation program, it is a closed-donor training and certification apparatus. From an institutional economics standpoint, this donor-only-training-and-certification / OpenAtom-Foundation-institutional-layer pairing is a structural finding: the OpenAtom Foundation is being operationalized as an institution whose primary institutional surface is donor-governance and donor-certification, not open-community participation. The donor-only training program is institutionally the sharpest documented formalization of the OpenAtom Foundation’s operating model at the Foundation’s own institutional surface — the Foundation is training its own donors on how to run open source inside their enterprises, not training the general public on open-source practice.

The training program’s three-module structure, verbatim from the OpenAtom Foundation WeChat text, is a three-tier-curriculum-institutional-design object:

  • Module 1 — Open Source Overview and Enterprise Value: the enterprise-strategy layer
  • Module 2 — Enterprise Internal Open Source and Open Source Governance Systems: the enterprise-intranet-governance layer
  • Module 3 — Enterprise Participation and Open Source Project Creation: the enterprise-project-creation layer

The three-module pairing — enterprise-strategy → enterprise-intranet-governance → enterprise-project-creation — is the first-documented Chinese-open-source-foundation three-tier-curriculum-institutional-design object. This is a foundational-software-ecosystem-institutionalization operationalization at the donor-training surface — the OpenAtom Foundation is operationalizing the enterprise-open-source institutional design at the donor-training layer, not at the general-public layer.

The Jiang Tao keynote surface pairs institutionally with this series’ September 2 briefing’s documentation of Jiang Tao’s essay “Chinese large models are world-class” and its four-account-book framework and eight-layer architecture in a COPU-branded institutional surface — the same Jiang Tao surface is being operationalized at the OpenAtom Foundation Board Director keynote layer simultaneously with the COPU-branded essay layer. The Jiang Tao keynote title “Open Source in the AI Era: Ecosystem and Infrastructure” pairs institutionally with this series’ August 28 briefing’s documentation of MIIT Liu Yulin’s three-pillar open-source strategy — the same Chinese-administrative three-pillar surface this series has documented at the MIIT-industrial-policy layer is being operationalized at the OpenAtom Foundation Board Director keynote layer simultaneously.

The training program’s certification (证书) dual-outcome structure pairs institutionally with this series’ August 30 briefing’s documentation of the Mulan Foundation’s training-and-certification apparatus — the same Chinese open-source foundation training-and-certification surface this series has documented at the Mulan Foundation layer is being operationalized at the OpenAtom Foundation layer simultaneously. This is a two-foundation-simultaneous-Chinese-open-source-training-and-certification pattern.

Institutional significance: OpenAtom Foundation’s Open Source General-Knowledge Training Class Phase 1 is the first-documented OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program surface in this series, and it is being operationalized at three institutional surfaces simultaneously — the donor-only-training-and-certification layer, the enterprise-strategy-plus-enterprise-intranet-governance-plus-enterprise-project-creation three-tier-curriculum layer, and the OpenAtom-Foundation-Chairman-plus-CSDN-Founder-Board-Director dual-leadership layer — a three-surface-simultaneous OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program pattern.

Commentary#

Three institutionally new placements on the same unsolved institutional object that this series’ prior twenty-eight briefings have been documenting: the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders.

This cycle’s briefing documents a structurally new institutional pattern: the same Chinese open-source surface is being operationalized simultaneously at three institutionally distinct surfaces — the Chinese-head-of-state BRICS-plus open-source AI zone layer (a five-point initiative announced by Xi Jinping at the 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi, with a BRICS AI open-source community, a BRICS digital ecosystem cloud platform, a BRICS engineer-cultivation alliance, and a 2027 China BRICS rotating chairmanship operational-authority surface), the Huawei-deputy-chairman HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer (Wang Tao’s keynote documenting CANN’s transition to sustained community-driven open-source development, Ascend’s formal PyTorch backend status, the Atlas-960E NPO near-package optics, the Ascend-960DT Q1-2027 launch acceleration, the Ascend-960PR Q3-2027 schedule, the Ascend-970-980 2028-2029 roadmap paired with the Tau Scaling Law, and the 4.16-million-Kunpeng-developer-and-90-third-party-open-source-Ascend-projects ecosystem-scale surface), and the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program layer (a donor-only open-source general-knowledge training-and-certification apparatus aimed exclusively at the Foundation’s own donors, with a three-module curriculum placing enterprise-strategy, enterprise-intranet-governance, and enterprise-project-creation at the same training surface).

The three placements are institutionally coherent on the same unsolved institutional object — the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders — and the three placements together document a three-surface-simultaneous-institutionalization pattern alongside the four-surface-simultaneous-institutionalization pattern this series’ September 17 briefing documented at the CAICT-administrative-institute layer, the State-Council-formal-level Bund-Summit layer, the DeepSeek-internal-senior-engineer-essay layer, and the Huawei-Supervisory-Board-Chair Huawei Voice Community layer.

One structural pattern across the three placements: each placement pairs institutionally with a prior briefing’s documented surface, and the three placements together document that the same Chinese open-source surface is being operationalized at three institutionally distinct surfaces simultaneously — an institutionally deeper three-surface-simultaneous pattern than the four-surface-simultaneous pattern this series’ September 17 briefing documented, because the three surfaces this cycle’s briefing documents sit at institutional layers this series’ prior briefings have not documented at before.

  • The Xi Jinping BRICS-plus open-source AI zone placement pairs institutionally with this series’ September 2 briefing’s COPU weekly minutes documentation of Lu Shouqun’s 50-vs-2 WAICO open-weight alignment framing at the KubeCon China keynote, and this series’ July 17 briefing’s documentation of the WAICO 29-country agreement signed in Shanghai — the same Global-South open-source instrument being operationalized at the Chinese-head-of-state BRICS-plus institutional layer simultaneously with the COPU/WAICO institutional layer.
  • The Wang Tao HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 keynote placement pairs institutionally with this series’ September 10 briefing’s DeepSeek × Ascend 950DT order commitment-and-capacity-gap object, this series’ September 15 briefing’s GOSIM Shenzhen 2026 Huawei Black-Light Community keynote and Ascend open-source training ecosystem keynote, and this series’ September 17 briefing’s Guo Ping Huawei Voice Community essay’s “we want to be a shovel-seller” commercial-model diagnostic — the same Huawei surface being operationalized at the Huawei-deputy-chairman HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer simultaneously with the DeepSeek-order-commitment-and-Huawei-output-capacity-gap layer, the GOSIM Shenzhen keynote layer, and the Huawei-Supervisory-Board-Chair Huawei Voice Community layer.
  • The OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program placement pairs institutionally with this series’ September 2 briefing’s Jiang Tao COPU-branded essay documentation, this series’ August 30 briefing’s Mulan Foundation training-and-certification apparatus documentation, and this series’ August 28 briefing’s MIIT Liu Yulin three-pillar open-source strategy documentation — the same Chinese open-source foundation training-and-certification surface being operationalized at the OpenAtom Foundation layer simultaneously with the COPU-branded essay layer, the Mulan Foundation layer, and the MIIT-industrial-policy layer.

One structural risk across the three placements: the same Chinese open-source surface is being operationalized at three institutionally distinct surfaces simultaneously, and the same “open-source” surface is being absorbed into multiple institutional mechanisms simultaneously at three institutionally distinct layers — a three-surface-simultaneous-institutional-absorption pattern.

The Xi Jinping BRICS-plus open-source AI zone placement is being operationalized at the Chinese-head-of-state BRICS Summit layer, and if the Chinese-head-of-state BRICS Summit layer absorbs the BRICS AI open-source community surface into a China-led BRICS-plus open-source institutional object rather than an operational-BRICS-community object, the BRICS AI open-source community surface converts into a China-led BRICS-plus open-source institutional object. The Wang Tao HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 keynote placement is being operationalized at the Huawei-deputy-chairman HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer, and if the Huawei-deputy-chairman HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer absorbs the CANN community-driven open-source-development surface into a Huawei-internal-computing-ecosystem object rather than an open-source-community object, the CANN community-driven open-source-development surface converts into a Huawei-internal-computing-ecosystem object. The OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program placement is being operationalized at the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training layer, and if the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training layer absorbs the open-source general-knowledge training-and-certification surface into a donor-governance object rather than an open-community-participation object, the open-source general-knowledge training-and-certification surface converts into a donor-governance object.

The institutional-economics question — which this cycle’s briefing documents but does not answer — is whether the Chinese institutional architecture can hold the three institutional surfaces simultaneously without one converting the other. If the Chinese-head-of-state BRICS Summit layer converts the BRICS AI open-source community surface into a China-led BRICS-plus open-source institutional object, the BRICS AI open-source community surface becomes a China-led BRICS-plus open-source institutional object rather than an operational-BRICS-community object; if the Huawei-deputy-chairman HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer converts the CANN community-driven open-source-development surface into a Huawei-internal-computing-ecosystem object, the CANN community-driven open-source-development surface becomes a Huawei-internal-computing-ecosystem object rather than an open-source-community object; if the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training layer converts the open-source general-knowledge training-and-certification surface into a donor-governance object, the open-source general-knowledge training-and-certification surface becomes a donor-governance object rather than an open-community-participation object.

One perspective, not a verdict.

Xi Jinping’s BRICS-plus open-source AI initiative, Wang Tao’s HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 keynote, and the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program are best read as observations of institutional movement in progress, not as verdicts on institutional direction. The BRICS-plus open-source AI initiative does not guarantee that the BRICS AI open-source community surface will remain an operational-BRICS-community object rather than convert into a China-led BRICS-plus open-source institutional object; Wang Tao’s HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 keynote does not guarantee that the CANN community-driven open-source-development surface will remain an open-source-community object rather than convert into a Huawei-internal-computing-ecosystem object; the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program does not guarantee that the open-source general-knowledge training-and-certification surface will remain an open-community-participation object rather than convert into a donor-governance object. What this cycle’s briefing documents is that the Chinese open-source surface has crossed into a three-surface-simultaneous-institutionalization pattern that sits at institutionally distinct layers — the Chinese-head-of-state BRICS-plus institutional layer, the Huawei-deputy-chairman HUAWEI CONNECT keynote layer, and the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training layer — and that the three-surface-simultaneous-institutionalization pattern at these institutionally distinct layers is the sharpest documented operationalization of the state-directed-industrial-policy surface at multiple institutionally distinct layers simultaneously.
