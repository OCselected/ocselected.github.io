Deepseek-Harness-Claude-Code-Mods-Plugin-Compatibility-Cui-Tianyi-Agentic-Infra-Institutional-Form

China Open Source Daily — 2026-10-10

Three institutionally dense placements on the same unsolved institutional object this series’ prior fifty briefings have been documenting — the pre-IPO governance form of a Chinese frontier AI lab — and all three placements this cycle’s briefing documents sit at institutional layers this series’ prior briefings have not documented at before. (1) Bloomberg’s October 6, 2026 reporting on DeepSeek’s approaching pre-IPO round of at least ¥80 billion (approximately US$11.93 billion), originally targeted at ¥50 billion with a target valuation of ¥500 billion (approximately US$74 billion), with China’s largest battery maker CATL and Tencent Holdings as the largest two committed investors, paired with Sina/RFI/iHeima’s re-reporting that DeepSeek is preparing to file for a STAR-board IPO in 2027 via CITIC Securities, paired with the Sina/Phoenix Tech piece’s documentation that the June 2026 first external round of approximately ¥50 billion-plus (approximately US$7.4 billion) was executed as a limited-partnership structure with external investors holding no voting rights and a five-year lockup while Liang Wenfeng personally contributed approximately ¥20 billion and retained close to 100 percent of voting rights. (2) Hongxing/Red Star News's October 6, 2026 reporting on Moonshot AI (月之暗面) completing its final pre-IPO round at a valuation of approximately US$50 billion, positioning for a Q1 2027 Hong Kong Exchange listing, paired with the Sina/Hongxing documentation that Moonshot AI has completed the business-registration change from “limited liability company” to “joint stock company” as a pre-IPO procedural signal, paired with the piece’s documentation of Moonshot’s six funding rounds in the first half of 2026 with valuation rising from US$10 billion to US$35 billion across January-to-July (with the final round lifting the valuation to approximately US$50 billion) and Moonshot's June 2026 annual recurring revenue (ARR) breaking through US$300 million on the back of Kimi K3’s launch. (3) Sina/Phoenix Tech’s documentation that during National Day holiday 2026 DeepSeek Harness released a new version with plugin and cross-platform enhancements including an experimental Claude Code Mods compatibility layer, paired with DeepSeek Harness head Cui Tianyi’s October 4, 2026 social-media statement that “the main purpose of building this compatibility layer is to verify whether the extension capabilities Claude Code Mods offers plugin authors are roughly a subset of the capabilities provided by DSH’s everything-is-a-plugin architecture”, and paired with Cui Tianyi’s own statement that “a fairly large portion of my daily working time is spent recruiting and interviewing for DeepSeek; DeepSeek’s goal is to achieve AGI, and DeepSeek Harness’s goal is to advance AGI from the Harness angle.” The institutional-economics question this cycle’s briefing documents but does not answer: whether the pre-IPO governance form of Chinese frontier AI labs — the DeepSeek-limited-partnership-plus-99-percent-voting-rights-plus-five-year-lockup structure and the Moonshot-limited-liability-to-joint-stock-conversion-plus-dual-track-Hong-Kong-and-Star-board-IPO structure — is the institutional-form object this series’ prior briefings’ Great Divergence 2.0 framework should be extended to cover, and whether the DeepSeek Harness Claude Code Mods compatibility-layer layer operationalizes the same open-source-vs-institutional-form boundary object this series’ prior briefings have documented at the OpenHarmony AI-tool-use-rulebook layer and the CANN-community-open-source-development layer.
