Xinchuang-Stratification-Era-Huawei-High-End-Vendor-Only-Beneficiary

China Open Source Daily — 2026-10-09

Two institutionally dense placements on the Chinese open-source institutional-form surface from the 2026-10-08 WeChat input feed, both of which sit at institutional layers this series’ prior briefings have not documented at before, plus one vendor-official-roundtable placement that pairs structurally with the same two. (1) OpenHarmony’s 《生成式AI工具使用与开源贡献策略》 (Generative AI Tool Use and Open Source Contribution Strategy) published by the 开放原子 (OpenAtom) Foundation’s 开源鸿蒙 community channel on 2026-10-08 — eight-section rulebook covering basic principles, permitted-use scenarios, disclosure norms, six prohibited behaviors, license/privacy/data rules, issue-bug-security-vulnerability special rules, reviewer-side rules, and community-enforcement-iteration — is the first-documented instance of a large Chinese OS-community official channel publishing a formal AI-tool-use rulebook for open-source contributions, positioning the same “large OS community” surface this series’ September 8 briefing documented at the 14-billion-device-OpenHarmony base-layer layer at the AI-governance-rule-execution layer simultaneously with the community’s vendor-foundation-separation-and-90-percent-to-70-percent-contribution-share-decline institutional object. (2) 国产信创外传 (Chinese state-informatization industry outlet) publishes a two-piece piece arguing that a wave of enterprises, government, and energy-sector units have slowed one-size-fits-all state-informatization (Xinchuang) replacement, retreating from replacement-rate-first KPIs toward business-first, risk-controlled value-based rollout, and pairing this with an article characterizing Huawei as the sole beneficiary in the Xinchuang stratification period. (3) The 自主可控新鲜事 (Autonomous-Controllable Fresh News) WeChat piece re-published Richard Yu’s September 29, 2026 international-media roundtable — where Yu simultaneously states that Huawei has “essentially broken free” from US technology dependency while stating that China still relies on DUV lithography — with the outlet’s selective headline-framing (emphasizing “essentially broken free” and weakening “still relies on DUV”) being a structurally new first-documented instance in this series of the autonomous-controllable narrative’s market-media-plus-administrative-narrative selective-transmission mechanism being operationalized at the individual-vendor-vs-national-industry dichotomy layer.
