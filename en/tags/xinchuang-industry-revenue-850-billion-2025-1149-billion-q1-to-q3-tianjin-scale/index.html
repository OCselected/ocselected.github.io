Xinchuang-Industry-Revenue-850-Billion-2025-1149-Billion-Q1-to-Q3-Tianjin-Scale

China Open Source Daily — 2026-10-13

Two institutionally dense placements, both from the 2026-10-12 WeChat input feed, sitting at institutional layers this series’ prior fifty-two briefings have not documented at before. (1) China Academy of Information and Communications Technology’s (CAICT, 中国信息通信研究院, directly under the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology) launch of the 2026 Internet Agent Governance Innovation Application Practice Activity on 2026-10-12 — the first-documented instance in this series of a Chinese state-adjacent research institute operationalizing the 2026-05-08 agent guidelines (Cyberspace Administration of China, NDRC, MIIT, jointly issued) at the institute-level application-activity layer: six recommendation directions spanning business-application plus infrastructure plus management-platform plus terminal plus inter-agent-interaction (explicitly naming the ATH agent trusted-handshake protocol plus A2A plus MCP) plus security-governance (explicitly naming open-source-community-governance-practice as a governance sub-topic), ten participant unit categories, single-unit cap of three recommendation items, submission window 2026-10-12 to 2026-11-06, review in 2026-11, results release at early 2026-12 AI-plus-Industry-Ecology Conference Agent Forum — completing the policy-framework-plus-technology-standard-plus-capability-assessment-plus-governance-practice institutional chain at its first application-layer activity layer, hosted by CAICT Cloud and Big Data Institute’s Open Source and Intelligent Safety Department (开源与智能安全部). Paired with this series’ prior October briefings’ documentation of the CAICT ATH protocol at the 2026-04-29-initiation-plus-AtomGit-plus-GitHub-dual-open-source-release-plus-2026-06-07-first-assessment-13-firms-plus-6-rounds-institutional-form-plus-talent-exchange layer — the pair completes the CAICT agent governance institutional object at the policy-framework-to-technology-standard-to-capability-assessment-to-governance-practice institutional-chain-completion layer. (2) The 自主可控新鲜事 outlet’s 2026-10-12 report “Tianjin quietly set up a Xinchuang Industry Bureau” (天津悄悄设了个信创产业局) documents, for the first time in this series, the Xinchuang institutional-formation institutional surface being operationalized at the renaming-plus-expansion-not-launch layer simultaneously with the administrative-bureau-plus-comprehensive-party-committee-plus-industry-alliance three-layer-architecture layer: the Tianjin Binhai High-Tech Development Zone Xinchuang Industry Bureau (天津滨海高新技术产业开发区信创产业局) has issued a batch of procurement pre-notices but no public inauguration record; historical names trace the institution from Marine-Economy-and-Military-Civilian-Fusion Industry Investment Bureau (2021 financial statements, still named 信创产业招商局 Investment Bureau) through an unnamed 2021-to-2023 transition window, to the March 2023 official appointment notice that first uses the current name — no public launch ceremony, no dedicated news release, only an institutional surface being re-oriented through name-change; the department’s financial statements disclose seven core functions including coordination of the Haihe Laboratory’s advanced-computing-and-key-software-Xinchuang research platform construction (the Haihe Laboratory adopts a small-core-large-platform model linking universities and enterprises across the base-research-to-industrialization chain); compared with the four-nationwide-categories of Xinchuang governance institutions (dedicated sections in Chengdu and Shanxi, working groups in Jiangsu and Zhejiang, Ningbo’s November 2024 service center, Beijing and Fujian department-coordination models), Tianjin picks the most institutionalized form; the Binhai Hi-Tech Zone has layered a Xinchuang Industry Comprehensive Party Committee over the Xinchuang Industry Bureau, absorbing Party-member technical backbones from research institutes and leading enterprises (Phytium 飞腾, Kylin 麒麟, Hygon 海光, Nandasoft 南大通用, Sugon 曙光, 360) into a three-layer architecture (administrative bureau + comprehensive Party committee + industry alliance); the scale metrics — 2024 Xinchuang industry revenue 72 billion yuan (up 30.1% YoY), 2025 full-year Xinchuang industry revenue approximately 85 billion yuan (up 17.4% YoY), 2025 Q1-to-Q3 Tianjin citywide Xinchuang industry scale 114.99 billion yuan, 2026 Q1 41 new landed enterprises registered at the Xinchuang Bureau. The two stories together document that two institutional objects this series’ prior October briefings have documented at the state-adjacent-research-institute-agent-governance-technology-standard layer and the Xinchuang-institute-and-Party-committee-plus-bureau-plus-alliance-institutional-formation layer are now being operationalized at the institute-level-application-activity-launch-and-most-institutionalized-Xinchuang-bureau institutional-form layer.
