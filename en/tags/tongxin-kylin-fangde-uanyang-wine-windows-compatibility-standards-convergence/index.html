Tongxin-Kylin-Fangde-Uanyang-Wine-Windows-Compatibility-Standards-Convergence

China Open Source Daily — 2026-09-25

Three institutionally dense placements on the same unsolved institutional object this series’ prior thirty-six briefings have been documenting — the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders. (1) WeChat 早安胡同 (2026-09-24): 统信软件 (Tongxin / UOS) and 银河麒麟软件 (Kylin) — the two leading desktop operating system vendors in the China Center for Information Security (CCIC) 安全可靠测评准入制 (Security-and-Reliability Assessment Access) regime — have both brought the 方德鸳鸯火锅 (Fangde Uanyang / 方德鸳鸯火锅 V8.0) compatibility platform into their respective application stores, with 100+ industrial-software and creative-design-specific software titles passing compatibility testing for one-click download from the app stores, and the platform based on a deep modification of open-source Wine to address legacy-industrial-software and hardware-peripheral compatibility. Previously Tongxin and Kylin had each been developing their own Windows-compatibility approaches with version-fragmentation and inconsistent user experience; now both are unified on the same compatibility layer, which the source frames as the Xinchuang desktop market transitioning from 各自为战造轮子 (each-fighting-on-their-own-and-re-inventing-the-wheel) to 分工协作 (division-of-labor collaboration), OS vendors concentrating on the OS itself, CCIC-certification, security-hardening, and channel coverage, and the compatibility-layer vendor concentrating on Windows-ecosystem migration and industrial-software and old-peripheral plug-in hard problems. This is the first-documented operationalization of the Jiashu Investment chairman-consolidation move (this series’ September 9 briefing) at a joint-product-milestone-and-division-of-labor layer, and the sharpest documented operationalization of the two-routes framework — compatibility-school (统信 + 麒麟 + 方德) 守存量 (retention of the stock) plus full-stack-school (Huawei HarmonyOS) 攻增量 (attack of the increment) — this series’ September 11 briefing documented at the Xinchuang-Lutoushe half-year scorecard layer. (2) Global Times via Yahoo Finance / PRNewswire (2026-09-18): Chinese AI startup Moonshot AI released its Kimi K3 large language model ahead of the opening of the 2026 World AI Conference and High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance — the world’s largest open-source model by parameter count to date — and a researcher commentary quoted in the same Global Times piece links the K3 launch to Xi Jinping’s speeches at the China Academy of Engineering (CAE) plenary and the 11th National Congress of the China Association for Science and Technology in July 2026 on 国家支持 and 优化评价体系 (national support and optimized evaluation systems) enabling young talent to pursue long-term breakthroughs. This is the first-documented instance in this series of a Chinese-frontier-lab-open-weight-model being operationalized at a state-science-policy-talent-evaluation-layer simultaneously with the WAIC-plus-High-Level-Meeting-on-Global-AI-Governance layer, and it pairs with this series’ September 23 briefing’s documentation of Kimi K3 at the open-weight-license-plus-commercial-monetization-threshold layer, and with this series’ September 24 briefing’s documentation of DeepSeek and Moonshot at the UN-Security-Council-AI-briefing-invitation layer. (3) OpenAtom Foundation WeChat (2026-09-24): Open Source General-Knowledge Training Class Phase 1 (企业开源通识研修班第一期) completed in Beijing September 23–24, 2026 with 45 donor companies, 120+ students, 9 expert lecturers, and 82 students passing the Open-Source-General-Knowledge-Certification exam — closing the learning-loop from 课程学习 to 互动研讨 to 能力认证 (course learning → interactive seminar → capability certification). OpenAtom Chairman Xie Shaofeng’s opening remarks placed 人才是开源事业持续发展的根本支撑 (talent is the fundamental support for the continued development of the open-source cause); CSDN founder Jiang Tao framed the AI-era developer ecosystem thesis as 代码变便宜信任变昂贵 (code becomes cheap, trust becomes expensive); Huawei Chief Open-Source Liaison Ren Xudong framed 开源是一种能力需要自上而下和自下而上双向驱动 (open source is a capability that requires two-way top-down and bottom-up driving); PingCAP Vice President Liu Song framed 开源不是商业模式而是生产方式与分发方式 (open source is not a business model but a mode of production and distribution). This is the completion-and-outcome documentation of the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training program this series’ September 18 briefing documented at the announcement layer, and it is the first-documented operationalization of the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training-and-certification apparatus at a closed-loop-completion layer with a specific donor-companies-students-experts-certified four-part scale surface and a three-module-curriculum surface (enterprise-strategy-plus-value → enterprise-intranet-governance-plus-open-source-governance-system → enterprise-participation-plus-open-source-project-creation).
